Our story

Small beginnings, strong together

Unity Credit Union (UCU) began ten years ago with only four members. Today we serve {{ total_members }} active members who save every month and support each other with loans at fair rates.

We work as a susu union with our own way of operating. Members pay a monthly contribution. Our year runs from 1 September to 31 August, and at the end of each year members receive their interest or dividend.

To keep the union strong for everyone, partial withdrawals are not allowed. Members are always free to exit.
